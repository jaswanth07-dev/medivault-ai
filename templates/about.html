ABOUT MEDIVAULT AI

A thoughtful place to organize medical documents.

MediVault AI is an educational document organization project. It helps people collect records, inspect extracted details, and revisit saved documents—not interpret health or make care decisions.

⌕

Purpose

Turn document intake into a clear, reviewable workflow. Original files remain available so users can compare extracted fields with their sources.

◉

Privacy approach

Accounts and records are stored locally using SQLite and workspace files. Records are scoped to their account in this application. This is not a hosted or audited healthcare platform.

⚕

Limitations

Text extraction and OCR can miss or misread information. Summaries are generated from structured fields and may be incomplete. Always verify with the original document and a qualified clinician.

Not medical advice: MediVault AI does not diagnose patients, assess results, or recommend medications, treatment, or medication changes. It is not a medical device.
SAFE TO EXPLORE

Sample records are fictional.

The demo loader creates five clearly labeled synthetic PDFs for testing the workflow. They are not real patient records.
