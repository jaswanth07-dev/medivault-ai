PRIVACY & DATA

How this workspace stores information

  • Account details, password hashes, session hashes, and document metadata are stored in the configured SQLite database.
  • Original uploads are kept as files in the local workspace storage directory.
  • Records are scoped to your account by the application. This local project is not a cloud backup service or audited healthcare platform.
  • There is no in-app bulk deletion or export function. To remove data, stop the app and manage the local database/files directly; avoid deleting files until you understand which records refer to them.

The app does not send document contents to an external AI service. OCR depends on the locally installed Tesseract tool.
